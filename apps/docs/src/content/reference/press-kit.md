The mark is the Angular shield with three streams running through it. The
streams cross out of order: each passes over one neighbour and under the other,
the way a streamed page's parts arrive in whatever order they are ready.

Every file below is in [`press-kit/`](../../../../../press-kit) in the
repository; its [README](../../../../../press-kit/README.md) lists them one by
one.

it: an avatar, an app icon, a header. Below 32 pixels use the small cut, which
keeps two of the streams and one crossing.

## Colours

The symbol is filled with one gradient, from red at its left edge through
magenta to violet at its right, rising slightly. The name is set in ink on
light backgrounds and in white on dark ones.

## Typeface

The name is set in [Geist](https://vercel.com/font): `angular-` in Regular,
`stream` in Bold. In the files it is drawn as outlines, so nothing depends on
the font being installed.

## Using it

- Keep clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of the symbol's height.
- Show the symbol at 32 pixels or more and the small cut below that, down to 16.
  Show the horizontal logo at 32 pixels high or more, and the stacked one at 80.
- On a photograph, a pattern or a mid-tone colour, use the white or the black
  version rather than the gradient.
- Do not recolour it, redraw its gradient, stretch it, rotate it, outline it or
  add effects to it, and do not set the name in another typeface.

## Where the shield comes from

The shield's outline is that of the [Angular icon](https://angular.dev/press-kit),
which the Angular team publishes under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and invites the
community to adapt. Here it is drawn as its six sides, each one straight line:
the shoulders carry on up to a point where the icon's letter comes through, and
the lower edges run straight to the tip. The streams are cut through it, and
the colours are its own. Angular is a trademark of Google LLC;
angular-stream is a community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by
Google or the Angular team.
